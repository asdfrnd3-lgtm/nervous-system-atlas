## 🔍 What Is nervous-system-atlas?

Imagine holding the entire human nervous system in your hands - rotating it, zooming into tiny structures, and seeing exactly how each part connects. That's what **nervous-system-atlas** offers, right in your web browser. 

This is not just a collection of images. It's a fully interactive 3D atlas containing **592 detailed brain and nerve structures**, all aligned to the internationally recognized MNI152 space used by doctors and researchers worldwide. Whether you're a medical student, a curious learner, or a healthcare professional refreshing your knowledge, this tool brings neuroanatomy to life.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🌐 Fully 3D Interactive Viewer
- **Rotate, zoom, and pan** through realistic brain structures with your mouse or touchscreen.
- Click any structure to see its name and read a detailed clinical explanation.
- Toggle layers on and off to see how different systems relate.

### 🧩 Synchronized MRI Slices
- As you move through the 3D model, **real MRI cross-sections** update in real-time.
- See exactly where each structure sits on an actual brain scan - perfect for understanding radiology.

### 🩸 Arterial Territories & Traced Pathways
- Visualize which parts of the brain are fed by each major artery.
- Follow tracing of key nerve pathways (like motor or sensory tracts) from start to finish.

### 🏥 Syndrome & Lesion Mode
- Simulate what happens when a specific area is damaged.
- See which clinical syndromes (like Broca's aphasia or parkinsonism) correspond to specific lesions.
- Understand *why* a stroke in one region causes specific symptoms.

### 📚 Clinical Topics & Glossary
- Browse 12 major clinical topics, from stroke to multiple sclerosis.
- **120+ glossary terms** defined in plain language.

### 🎯 Built-in Quiz Mode
- Test yourself with 50+ multiple-choice questions.
- Get instant feedback with explanations linked back to the 3D model.
- Track your improvement over time.

### 🌍 Bilingual Support
- Complete interface and content in **both English and Turkish**.
- Switch languages at any time with one click.
